# 2026-08-20 · 12 · Declining out-of-scope BMAD skills mid-session

- **Tool**: Claude Code (desktop)
- **Model**: Claude Fable 5
- **Phase**: Planning (methodology familiarization)
- **Intent**: Confirm the policy when a BMAD agent proposes skills outside the planned
  chain (e.g. brainstorming).

## Prompt (verbatim)

> ok si el agente me proponer usar otra skills que estan fuera de lo que planificamos, como por ejemplo bmad braisntorming, mejor le pongo fuera de scope porque puede demorar y no aportar nada. verdad.

## Outcome

- Confirmed, with one nuance. General rule: discover-the-product skills (brainstorming,
  market-research, domain-research, PRFAQ) are declined — the product is pre-scoped by
  the challenge brief and the brief explicitly zeroes the value of domain knowledge.
- Nuance: don't blanket-refuse — apply the guard question. A short, targeted
  *advanced elicitation* pass on a weak section (e.g. PRD edge cases, confidence-flag
  semantics) deepens an answer we need and is allowed sparingly.
- Technique: decline *with the reason stated in-session* so the reasoned refusal lands
  in the transcript — Independent-Judgment evidence.
- `plan/02` "Deliberately skipped" section expanded to name these skills and the policy.
