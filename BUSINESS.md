# BUSINESS.md

I would charge the platform that onboards restaurants **€2 per menu processed**, and I would ship it today as an internal tool behind the platform's login — not yet as a paid feature, and never as unreviewed automation. What is sold is the review record: one menu in, one set of rows out, each with a `reliable`/`uncertain` flag and the evidence quoted under it. Cost is settled: $0.0069 for a real 34-dish menu on `gpt-5.6-luna` (measured 2026-08-22; the terra tier costs 9× and did not improve the flag), so the margin at €2 is above 99% once the input is capped — the cap is not built yet, and without it one 10 MB text source bills about $0.50 per attempt. Value is not settled: the anchor is the operator's 15–30 minutes per menu (the persona's number, not measured), €7.50–15 at a loaded €30 per hour, and €2 takes a quarter of that saving only if the review is faster than the typing it replaces; on the two real menus every row came back `uncertain` (38 of 38, B42), so the tool removed the typing and none of the reading. €2 holds while transcription is in that range and the platform wants the record; if it wants rows only, there is nothing to charge beyond cost. Liability sets the rest of the price: a wrong allergen in a `reliable` row is a safety event, and the measurement found one way it happens — an ingredient word quoted as a declaration, 6 of 34 rows (B45) — so the brief's "zero false reliables by construction" is retracted to "by rule, given a model that honours its own labels". The conditions: internal, after three fixes in `core/` measured in hours; paid, after auth, rate limits, the cap, a retry on 429 and verdicts that survive a re-run — two to three weeks — and after a week of timing the review on real menus. The three fixes were not made before submission: two days out, with one test that does not cover the visual path, the choice was to record them (D8, D28).
