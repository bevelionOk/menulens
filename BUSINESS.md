# BUSINESS.md

I would charge the platform that onboards restaurants **€0.50 per menu processed**, and I would ship it today as an internal tool behind the platform's login — not yet as a paid feature, and never as unreviewed automation. What is sold is the review record: one menu in, one set of rows out, each with a `reliable`/`uncertain` flag and the evidence quoted under it. Cost is settled: $0.0069 of model for a real 34-dish menu on `gpt-5.6-luna` (measured 2026-08-22; $0.0063 on the 23rd after the fixes below; the terra tier costs 9× and did not improve the flag), plus about €0.05 of infrastructure per menu at 500 menus a month — €0.06 in all; at 50 a month the infrastructure alone is €0.50 and the price covers cost and nothing else. Value is not: the anchor was the operator's 15–30 minutes per menu, a persona's number nobody measured, and on the two real menus every row came back `uncertain` (38 of 38, B42), so the tool removed the typing and none of the reading. A saving of 5–10 minutes is €2.50–5 at €30 per hour; the €2 first written here (D28) took 40–80 % of it, and a document-extraction API charges about $0.10 for the same three pages without a review record. €0.50 is eight times the cost, 10–20 % of the saving, and holds while the platform wants the record; if it wants rows only, there is nothing to charge beyond cost (D29). The price moves to €1–2 when two things are measured on real menus: the review time, and a `reliable` share above about 30 % — which needs the menu's legend codes read as declarations (B42). Liability sets the rest: a wrong allergen in a `reliable` row is a safety event. The measurement found one way it happened — an ingredient word quoted as a declaration, 6 of 34 rows on one run of the same PDF that gave 0 of 34 that morning (B45, B46) — so the brief's "zero false reliables by construction" is retracted to "by rule, given a model that honours its own labels", and the rule that was missing now exists: a declaration needs a declaration marker (fixed 2026-08-23 with B10 and B14, reviewed; B45 re-measured, the other two pinned in the one test). The conditions: internal, now; paid, after auth, rate limits, an input cap at 200 k characters (without it one 10 MB text source bills about $0.50 per attempt), a retry on 429 and verdicts that survive a re-run — two to three weeks — and after a week of timing the review on real menus.
